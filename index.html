01 Foundation · The four pillars

A behaviour-driven system, not just an app.

Every screen, every notification, every tap is engineered to nudge meaningful action across four interlocking pillars.

01

Living

Payment, access, facility booking, in-home services and the property documents that matter — all in one tap.

02

Commerce

Tenant campaigns, QR-scan promotions, mall directory and a discovery feed that turns footfall into transactions.

03

Engagement

Community events, neighbour-to-neighbour interactions, announcements, and a feed that makes the place feel alive.

04

Loyalty

One points engine across every tenant. Earn living, spend visiting — building cross-merchant value that rewards return.
